# Strategy Principles

An undercut attempts to gain track position by stopping first and using the faster pace of
fresh tyres before a rival stops. Its value depends on warm-up, pit loss, traffic after the
stop, and the rival's remaining tyre performance.

An overcut extends the current stint while the rival stops. It is useful when the current
tyres retain pace, the new compound warms slowly, or the early stopper rejoins in traffic.

Pit-window calculations must separate observed facts from assumptions. Green-flag pit loss,
future degradation, traffic delay, weather, and safety-car probability are estimates and
should be shown explicitly rather than folded into an unexplained recommendation.
